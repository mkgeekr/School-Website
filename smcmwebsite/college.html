COLLEGE

SMCM's College programs prepare graduates for meaningful careers and further study, combining rigorous coursework with real-world experience, internships, and mentorship from faculty active in their fields.

The College Department envisions its graduates as educated Christians, living the life of service and excellence, as they work towards preserving their identity as Filipino Marians.

Within the context of a community of responsible Christian leaders and given an enriched program with a well-balanced curriculum, the SMCM College Department aims to develop graduates who:

  • Exemplify professional excellence and remarkable leadership abilities;
  • Apply the knowledge, skills and attitudes learned towards the betterment of society especially the poor;
  • Live a life that is anchored on Christian principles and teachings; and
  • Can manifest competence and adaptability in any setting with his intellectual, technical and interpersonal skills.
