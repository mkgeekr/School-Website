FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

When does the application window open?

Admissions and enrollment are announced by SMCM for each school year. Students and parents are encouraged to check the official school announcements for the latest enrollment schedules and application information. Online enrollment is also available through the SMCM Student Portal.

Is there an entrance examination?

Yes. Applicants undergo an entrance assessment as part of the admission process. For Senior High School applicants, SMCM also conducts interviews to help determine proper placement and advises students on strand options based on their academic performance and career interests.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. SMCM provides scholarship opportunities for deserving students. The school has opened scholarship grants to qualified students, including deserving students from public schools, as part of its commitment to making quality education accessible.
