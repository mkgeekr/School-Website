SENIOR HIGH

SMCM's Senior High School program builds on the foundation set in Junior High, guiding students toward their chosen academic strand while sharpening the skills, discipline, and character needed for college and beyond.

Students may choose from a range of academic strands designed to match their interests, strengths, and future goals. Each strand combines specialized subjects with a strong general education foundation, allowing students to explore their chosen field while developing critical thinking, communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills.

Beyond the classroom, Senior High students are encouraged to take part in organizations, competitions, leadership opportunities, community initiatives, and other co-curricular activities. These experiences help students develop confidence, independence, and a sense of responsibility as they prepare for university, employment, and the wider world.
