STUDENT COUNCIL

The Marian Student Council represents the student body, organizing campus events, voicing student concerns to faculty and administration, and building leadership skills among its elected officers.

Throughout the school year, the MSC organizes a variety of activities that bring the student body together, from orientation programs and intramural sports to cultural celebrations and fundraising drives. Beyond events, the council serves as the official channel through which students can raise concerns about academic policies, facilities, and campus life, ensuring that student voices are heard in discussions with faculty and administration.

Serving as an officer in MSC offers students hands-on experience in governance, communication, and teamwork. Elected members learn to manage budgets, coordinate committees, and represent their peers in decision-making processes, skills that extend well beyond their time in office and prepare them for future roles in their careers and communities.
