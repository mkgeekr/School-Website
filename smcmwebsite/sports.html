SPORTS

SPORTS

At St. Mary's College of Meycauayan, sports and physical education are an important part of the Ignacian-Marian formation we give our students — building discipline, teamwork, and healthy competition alongside academic excellence.

Students take part in Siglaro, SMCM's annual sportsfest, where Marians from every department come together to compete, cheer each other on, and celebrate the unbreakable Marian spirit. Games and activities are held at the College's Multipurpose Hall, which serves as the venue for our sports events and gatherings throughout the school year.

Whether on the court or on the field, SMCM encourages every student-athlete to give their best — because at SMCM, win or lose, our Marians play with faith, teamwork, and heart.
