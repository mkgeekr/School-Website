ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Our Philosophy

SMCM welcomes students who demonstrate academic potential, integrity, curiosity, leadership, and a commitment to personal growth. Admission is competitive and based on a comprehensive review of each applicant.

Requirements by Level

a. Preschool

  • Photocopy of Birth Certificate from the National Statistics Office (original copy to be presented)
  • Photocopy of Catholic Baptismal Certificate (original copy to be presented)
  • One (1) pc. 1″ x 1″ colored I.D. picture
  • Alien Certificate of Residency if the student is a foreigner

Age Requirements:
Junior Kinder   : 4 years old by October 31
Senior Kinder   : 5 years old by October 31

b. Grade 1 – Grade 6

  • Photocopy of Birth Certificate from the National Statistics Office (original copy to be presented)
  • Photocopy of the Catholic Baptismal Certificate (original copy to be presented)
  • One (1) pc. 1″ x 1″ colored I.D. picture
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Satisfactory rating in the entrance examination and the final interview

c. Junior High School Level

A. Grade Seven and New Students

  • Report Card reflecting the 1st to 3rd quarter with no failing grades
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character duly signed by the Principal
  • With an average grade of 80% in conduct or its equivalent
  • Photocopy of the Baptismal Certificate & PSA Birth Certificate
  • 1 pc 2x2 I.D. picture
  • Photocopy of the Form 137-A or Certification of Subjects taken with corresponding units earned (For transferees only)

B. Admission Procedure

  1. Submit the requirements to the Registrar’s Office.
  2. Follow the entire enrolment procedure.

For Old Students

  • Report card
  • Students who are recommended for Probation by the Committee on Discipline/Academic may seek re-admission after signing the agreement by parents/guardian and the student.
  • Students who will be retained in the same level will not be re-admitted if failed the same subject/s twice.
  • Students who have been recommended for transfer / dismissal due to grave misconduct/incorrigibility and have been subjected to disciplinary action will not be re-admitted.

d. Senior High School Level

For Grades 11-12

For new students and transferees, they must submit the following:

  • Original Report card
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Photocopy of Baptismal and PSA Birth Certificate
  • QVR/ESC Certificate for Grade 10 completers from private schools
  • Students are not permitted to take any subjects not within the pre-scribed semester or any advanced subject without prior assessment of the Senior High School Coordinator and approval of the Principal and Vice President for Academics

Graduating Students (Grade 12)

  1. Application for graduation must be filed in the first term of the last academic year to allow the candidates to be informed of the deficiencies before enrolling for their last term.
  2. Graduating students must have completed all academic requirements to qualify as candidates for graduation as stipulated in DepEd No. 8, s. 2015.

B. Admission Procedure

  1. Submit the requirements to the Registrar’s Office.
  2. Follow the entire enrolment procedure.

For Old Students

e. College

New Students/Freshmen

  • Senior High School Report Card or Form 138
  • Original Copy of birth certificate from PSA
  • Marriage Certificate for married students
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • 4 pcs 2x2 pictures
  • 4 pcs long folder
  • 2 pcs long brown envelopes

Transferees

  • Honorable Dismissal/Transfer Credentials
  • Original copy of birth certificate from PSA
  • Marriage Certificate for married students
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Informative copy of Transcript of Records
  • 4 pcs 2x2 pictures
  • 4 pcs long folders
  • 2 pcs long brown envelopes

(Note: No 4th year level students will be accepted as transferees)

Cross-Enrollees

  • Cross-enrollment permit from the home school
  • Approval of the Registrar
  • 4 pcs 2x2 colored pictures with blue background
  • 2 pcs long brown envelopes
