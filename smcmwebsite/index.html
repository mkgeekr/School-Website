Mission & Vision

VISION

St. Mary’s College of Meycauayan is a Catholic Ignacian-Marian institution, nurturing and empowering learners towards ethical and social responsibility for global competence with exemplified life-long skills.

MISSION

True to our Ignacian-Marian identity, we commit ourselves to:

Grow deeper in discernment to be the bearers of hope in today’s world;

Foster learners who witness faith, service and excellence;

Pursue innovative programs, approaches and strategies to develop global professionals;

Build up resources, capabilities, and opportunities for quality life; and

Develop the school following Catholic doctrine.
