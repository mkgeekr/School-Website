INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS

Welcoming Students From Abroad

SMCM welcomes applicants from outside the country and provides additional guidance throughout the application process, including document equivalency, visa/study permit coordination, and orientation support once enrolled.

Additional Requirements

  • Photocopy of Passport (photo and personal information pages)
  • Equivalency of academic records, if issued outside the country
  • Proof of English or Filipino language proficiency, where applicable
  • Student visa or study permit documentation, once accepted
