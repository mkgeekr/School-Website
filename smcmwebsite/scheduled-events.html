As the semester draws to a close, SMCM's calendar is filled with important academic dates and community celebrations. From final exams to festive gatherings, here's what students can look forward to in the coming weeks.

  • Start of 1st Semester Final — Marks the beginning of the semester's final term.
  • 1st Semester Final Exams — The culmination of the semester's coursework, where students take their final assessments across all subjects to close out the term.
  • Christmas Party — A festive, school-wide celebration bringing students, faculty, and staff together to mark the holiday season with food, games, and performances.
  • 111th Foundation Anniversary of SMCM — A milestone celebration honoring 111 years of Saint Mary's College of Meycauayan's history, legacy, and contributions to the community.
