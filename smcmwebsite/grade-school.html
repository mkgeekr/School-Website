GRADE SCHOOL

GRADE SCHOOL

SMCM's Grade School program lays the foundation for a lifetime of learning. From Kindergarten through Grade 6, young learners are guided through a nurturing and structured environment where academic skills, values formation, and social-emotional growth develop hand in hand.

Our teachers use age-appropriate, hands-on approaches to build strong fundamentals in literacy, numeracy, science, and the arts, while instilling discipline, faith, and good character from an early age. Small class sizes and close guidance allow every child to be seen, supported, and encouraged to grow at their own pace.

Beyond the classroom, students take part in activities that build confidence and curiosity — from reading programs and creative projects to values formation sessions and community celebrations — preparing them for a smooth transition into Junior High School.
